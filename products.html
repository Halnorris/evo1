Property management, revolutionised.

A simple and coherent end-to-end solution that connects residents, trades, landlords and property managers to support the day-to-day management and maintenance of residential property portfolios — the smartest, most intuitive and convenient tenant and trades app on the market.

Evo is more than just repairs, it's knowing how homes and communities work. Our modern digital approach drives resident engagement, and provides helpful, useful information right where they need it.

Living AppResident digital logbook
Living App · For residents

A digital logbook for every home.

Our Living App is more than just a repair reporting tool — it's a resident's digital logbook for their home, providing secure storage for tenancy documents, easy-to-find user guides and property-specific FAQs to assist self-help.

  • Report problems
  • 24/7 emergency response
  • In-app messaging
  • Alerts and notifications
  • Easy appointment booking
Trades AppJob assignment & progress
Trades App · For tradespeople

Every job, communicated in full.

Our Trades App provides a simple and effective way for tradespeople to view and accept jobs. It ensures that all progress on a particular job is fully communicated to both the resident and the landlord.

  • On demand job assignment
  • Smart skill & location scheduling
  • Job updates with photos & videos
  • Alerts and notifications
  • In-app billing & payments
