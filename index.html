The platform

One connected platform, three purpose-built apps

Evo is more than repairs — it's knowing how homes and communities work. Our modern digital approach drives resident engagement and gives every stakeholder the information they need, right when they need it.

Living App

A resident's digital logbook for their home — secure document storage, user guides and property-specific FAQs, alongside fast, simple repair reporting.

  • Report problems in seconds
  • 24/7 emergency response
  • In-app messaging
  • Easy appointment booking
Explore Living App

Trades App

A simple, effective way for tradespeople to view and accept jobs — with progress fully communicated to both the resident and the landlord at every stage.

  • On-demand job assignment
  • Smart skill & location scheduling
  • Job updates with photos & video
  • In-app billing & payments
Explore Trades App

Dashboard

Empowers landlords and property managers to deliver an exceptional experience — full service history, real-time job tracking and insights that drive efficiency.

  • Secure online dashboard
  • People, places, jobs & notes
  • Knowledge base property logbooks
  • Real-time calendar
