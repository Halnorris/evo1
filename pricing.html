Questions

Frequently asked questions

How is pricing calculated?
Every plan is priced per property, per month — so cost scales predictably with the size of your portfolio, whether that's ten homes or ten thousand.
What's the difference between monthly and annual billing?
Annual billing is paid upfront for the year and carries a discount of roughly 10% versus paying monthly, across all three tiers.
Can I add Heating Protect or Home Protect to a Bronze plan?
Heating Protect and Home Protect are available as add-ons on Silver and Gold plans. Landlord Compliance is available as an add-on on any tier, including Bronze.
What minimum terms apply?
Bronze has a minimum 30-day term, Silver a minimum 12-month term, and Gold a minimum 24-month term — reflecting the depth of service included at each level.
Do you work with public sector organisations?
Yes — EVO is a Crown Commercial Service approved supplier and listed on the Digital Marketplace, so we can be procured through existing public sector frameworks.
